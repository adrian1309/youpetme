# YouPetMe — Project Overview

## Project

YouPetMe is an animal adoption platform designed to connect people looking to adopt animals with shelters and organizations that manage animals available for adoption.

The platform will combine animal adoption, collaboration with external shelters, pet-related services and AI-assisted experiences.

The project will be developed as part of a Master's Final Project (TFM) focused on AI-assisted software development.

---

## Main Objective

The main objective is to design and develop a scalable software platform that facilitates animal adoption while demonstrating a professional software engineering process supported by Artificial Intelligence.

The project will not only focus on the final application, but also on the development methodology, architecture, documentation, testing and use of AI development tools.

---

## Core Concept

YouPetMe will present its own catalogue of animals (managed internally in the MVP) and, in the future, will collaborate with external shelters and organizations.

The platform will allow users to:

- Discover animals available for adoption.
- Search and filter animals.
- View detailed information about animals.
- Receive personalized animal recommendations.
- Start an adoption process.
- View animals from external shelters (future).
- Discover collaborating businesses and veterinarians (future).
- Interact with an AI assistant.
- Manage their adopted animals (future).

---

## Future Vision

The platform may eventually include:

- Temporary pet care.
- Caregiver profiles.
- Veterinary services.
- Partner stores.
- Discounts for animals with special circumstances.
- Social content from adopters.
- AI-generated animal avatars.
- Personalized virtual environments for adopted animals.
- Mobile applications.
- Advanced AI-based matching.
- Additional integrations with shelters and organizations.

---

## Development Philosophy

The project will be developed incrementally.

The first objective is to create a well-defined MVP.

Future functionality will be separated into subsequent versions.

Ideas that are not part of the MVP must be documented rather than implemented prematurely.
